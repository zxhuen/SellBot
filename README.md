# SellBot

> AI-powered product assistants for marketplace listings.

SellBot enables sellers to generate a shareable AI assistant for any product they are selling. Instead of answering the same customer questions repeatedly, sellers can attach an AI chat link to their marketplace listing, allowing buyers to receive instant responses 24/7.

---

## Overview

Marketplace sellers often receive repetitive questions about their products, such as:

- Is this still available?
- Does it include the original box?
- What are the specifications?
- Is the price negotiable?
- What accessories are included?

SellBot solves this by generating an AI assistant for each product listing.

The seller provides the product information once, and the AI answers customer questions using only the provided product details.

---

-  AI-powered product assistants
